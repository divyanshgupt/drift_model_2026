The goal of this set of scripts is to check the following:

- The eigenvalues of the weight matrix
- If the network is in ISN regime

- Drift purely in the weight matrix
    - how much does the center of mass of each row (the incoming weights to a post synaptic neuron) shift over:
        - each stimulus presentation
        - and on average over multiple stimulus presentations


- The **firing rate regimes** as a function of weight strength for co-tuned vs random circuits
